
# Where Are My Shades?

## Comic Description
"Where Are My Shades?" is a short, four panel comic strip about a pet owner who cannot find their shades.

The owner searches around the house and even asks their pet turtle, Swag, if he has seen them. After the owner leaves for work, Swag discovers the missing shades and ends up wearing them while relaxing on the couch.

## Characters
- The Owner: The person searching for their missing shades, he likes to talk to swag all the time, he assumes that swag cannot understand him because he's just a turtle.
- Swag: A very smart pet turtle that can understand what his owner is saying but chooses not to speak back as that would probably freak him out.
